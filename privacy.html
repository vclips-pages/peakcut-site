Privacy

PeakCut keeps the owner's sign-in tokens on the owner's own computer only. Through TikTok it reads the list of the owner's own videos and their view counts. It collects nothing about anyone else, and shares nothing with anyone.

This website stores no data and uses no cookies. Its sign-in page only passes TikTok's answer on to PeakCut on the owner's own computer.

Removing the connection, in PeakCut or in TikTok's settings, ends it.
