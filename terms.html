Terms of use

PeakCut is a private tool run by its owner to make short clips of live streams and post them to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

With TikTok, PeakCut only reads the owner's own videos and their view counts, to show the owner how the posts are doing. It never posts, changes or deletes anything on TikTok through this connection.

The owner can end the connection at any time, in PeakCut or in TikTok's settings.
